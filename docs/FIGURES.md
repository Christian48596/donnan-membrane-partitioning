# Figures and source data

All five figures are generated by `code/make_figures.py` from exported numerical
tables. Source data are in `figures/source_data/`. Settings are in
`config/figure_style.json` and captions in `config/figure_captions.json`.

## Figure definitions

| Figure | Numerical source tables | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `fig1_screening_curve.csv`; `fig1_screening_ranges.csv` | Dilute Debye reference and calculated membrane ionic-strength ranges; only the reference curve is plotted |
| 2 | `fig2_sorption.csv` | Observed chloride/total sulfate and four model treatments |
| 3 | `fig3_residuals.csv`; `fig3_series_means.csv` | Concentration-dependent residuals and five-point series summaries |
| 4 | `fig4_identifiability.csv` | Prospective mechanism-detection power |
| 5 | `fig5_apparent_charge.csv`; `fig5_transferability.csv`; `fig5_exchange_curves.csv` | Charge fitting, cross-salt transfer and conditional Li/Na exchange |

## Symbols, legends and intervals

**Figure 1.** The grey region marks a dilute Debye length below the illustrative
ion diameter of 0.30 nm. This is a molecular-scale comparison rather than a
measured membrane screening length.

**Figure 2.** Open circles show measurements; vertical bars are reported standard
deviations. Each panel legend gives membrane, salt and Sujanani source year.
Chloride data are from 2021 and total sulfate from 2023. The green curves evaluate
the published weighted association closure with equal paired fractions in
both phases. Purple curves impose conditional species equilibrium.
Sulfate y values include free sulfate and pairs.

**Figure 3.** Panels (a) and (b) show ideal and Manning/Donnan residuals on the
same scales. Filled markers and solid lines denote Nafion 117; open markers
and dashed lines denote CR-61. Panel (c) shows a mean and minimum-to-maximum
range for each five-point model series. These whiskers describe concentration
dependence rather than uncertainty in the mean. Association entries apply to
the sulfate series.

**Figure 4.** Bars show simulated detection probabilities for independent
counter-ion and co-ion measurements. Whiskers are 95% Wilson intervals over
1500 trials. Blue bars use an exact baseline and orange bars include the
specified co-ion baseline-error response. These are prospective designs.

**Figure 5.** Panel (a) shows signed apparent charge on a symmetric logarithmic
scale; grey shading marks negative values. Panel (b) transfers the ideal charge
calibrated in 0.01 mol/L NaCl to MgCl2 and compares predictions with observed
chloride uptake. Panels (c,d) show conditional Li/Na exchange at `xi = 3.1`,
with unit free-ion preference and activity ratio. Coloured dashed lines are
the corresponding reservoir asymptotes.

## Artwork

Figures 1 and 4 are 83 mm wide; Figures 2, 3 and 5 are 171 mm wide.
Axis labels, ticks and legends are 9 pt. Panel letters are bold at 11 pt
and appear on the three multi-panel figures. Axis units and the chemical
formulas identify the plotted quantities. The two brief grey-zone labels
are "Below ion size" and "Negative apparent charge".

Nimbus Sans fonts and their licence are supplied in `assets/fonts/`.
PDF embeds the fonts; SVG retains editable text. PNG and LZW TIFF are 600 dpi.
Exports use fixed physical canvases. `figures/manifest.json` records dimensions,
minimum text size, input hashes and export hashes.

## Commands

After generating the numerical results:

```bash
python code/make_figures.py
python code/verify_outputs.py
```

For a separate low-resolution preview:

```bash
python code/make_figures.py --formats png --dpi 180 --output-dir preview
```
